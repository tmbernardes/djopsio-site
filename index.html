Library

A Rekordbox library you can trust before every gig.

Tagging, cues and file fixes are the hours nobody sees. DJOPS does them across your whole collection and writes everything back to Rekordbox.

  • Tags that mean something to a DJ: genre and sub-genre, energy, vocals, instruments and the moment of the night a track fits
  • Hot cues and loops on the moments you want, for every track, placed from its energy data
  • Any file to what club players accept, with cues and beatgrids kept. Free, always
  • Playlists built from your tags, plus Spotify sync and Beatport buy lists

Plan

Walk into every gig with a plan for that room.

Every gig lives in your gigs schedule, with its notes and set options. DJOPS builds the sets from your tagged tracks, their energy and styles, and from what you've already played.

  • A gigs schedule: date, venue, slot and vibe for every booking
  • Notes on every gig, from the booking details to what to remember on the night
  • Set options for each gig, so you can pick the one that fits the room when you get there
  • Build playlists fast from your tagged tracks: energy, style and the moment of the night each one fits
  • A read of your past sets: energy, peaks, and the tracks that moved the room

Produce

Your set, filmed, cut and published.

Drop in the recordings from the night. DJOPS syncs every camera to your clean audio and cuts the video to the music automatically. You just review it.

  • Multi-camera and 360 footage cut on the builds, drops and big moments
  • 360 reframed automatically to follow you behind the decks
  • Your board or mastered audio synced over the footage, replacing the camera mic
  • Thumbnail, title, tracklist and chapters written for you
  • Published to YouTube and SoundCloud, private first if you want to check it

Grow

Every gig becomes a week of posts.

It's all automatic: the moments that carried the room are cut, captioned and scheduled across your accounts. You just review and approve.

  • The best moments picked for you, cut vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, and wide for YouTube
  • Hooks, captions and a first comment written in your voice, for each platform
  • One calendar for every account and every artist you manage
  • The full set on YouTube and SoundCloud with title, tracklist, chapters and thumbnail
  • Channel insights: what worked, what didn't, and what to post next

FAQ

Questions DJs ask first

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Your library and recordings stay on your Mac, and the analysis runs there. Only the videos and posts you approve go out, and only to the platforms you choose.

What do I need?

A Mac (Apple Silicon recommended). The video and clips don't need Rekordbox: they work from your camera and audio recordings. Only the library and plan tools connect to Rekordbox.

Where can it publish?

YouTube and SoundCloud directly. Instagram and TikTok through Publer, a scheduling service you connect to DJOPS.

Will it post anything I haven't seen?

No. You review every video, title, thumbnail and clip, and nothing is published until you approve it.

What does it cost?

It's free during the beta. After launch it stays free to use: you'll see every result before paying anything, and you only pay when you export or publish.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. DJOPS is Mac only for its first year.
