Publishing to YouTube

Connect your own YouTube channel with Google sign-in. When you approve a set, DJOPS publishes it for you:

  • Uploads the full set and its short clips to the channel you pick
  • Writes the title and description, with the tracklist, artist credits and chapter timestamps
  • Sets the thumbnail it generated from your footage
  • Schedules the release and adds the set to a series playlist

DJOPS only acts on channels you connect, only after you review and approve, and you can disconnect at any time. Your sign-in stays on your Mac. How we handle your data.
