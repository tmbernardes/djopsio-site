Does it work without AI?

The video, clips and recap, covers, the Video Mixer, hot cues and the library tools run on your Mac. Hooks and captions come out plain, written from the set's details, and a gray note in the job window says when a run had no AI. Titles, descriptions, reports and channel questions need one.

Bring your own AI. Connect Claude, OpenAI or Gemini with your own key, or run a model on your Mac with LM Studio. AI writes titles, descriptions, hooks and captions, and suggests tags in Tag Pilot. Gemini has a free tier.

Do I need AI?

Does it work offline?

Your sets, clips and Rekordbox library live on your Mac, so most of the app runs with no internet. Sign-ins, uploads and posting, Google Drive, store lookups, Channel Studio and the cloud AIs wait for a connection.

What works offline

Where are my files backed up?

Rekordbox restore points. Before each write, DJOPS saves one. Roll your library and its exported XML back together from Backups. They cover the database, not your music files.

Google Drive. Uploads the folders you choose, and only what is new or changed. DJOPS can also keep a Drive copy of each set's final mix.

Your external SSD. Moves recordings off your Mac, and deletes the Mac copy only once the drive's copy matches in size and checksum. Your music and Rekordbox library are copied, never deleted.

Clip files for posting can live on your Google Drive or Cloudflare R2. Is my library backed up?

Will DJOPS change or mess up my Rekordbox library?

Only when you press a button that writes, and it backs up the Rekordbox database first. Rekordbox must be closed while it writes. The backup covers the database, not your music files.

Read the full answer

Will my sets and clips get copyright claims on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok?

They can, and on a full DJ set a claim is likely. DJOPS does not clear rights, cannot stop claims and does not try to hide music from matching. A claim is not a strike, but it can limit where a video plays.
