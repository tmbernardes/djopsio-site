Clips guidePreview

The moments the room will remember.

The drop, the second drop, the crowd's reaction. Cut, captioned and kept, so the best of every night is never forgotten.

Rules we learned rating our own clips, with real examples.

  1. Open on the DJ.
  2. Aim at the mixer, and zoom out enough to see the decks.
  3. Lean toward the side of the crowd that moves.
  4. Skip the moments where nothing happens behind the decks.

Tools in the app

Cue Studio

Hot cues, placed from the music.

It reads each track's beat grid and energy and suggests hot cues on the main moments, on bar starts. It backs up Rekordbox before it writes.

Illustration, not a real track. Cue Studio's default letters: A Groove entry · B 1st Break · C 1st Drop · E Vocal 1 · H 2nd Drop · D Mix-out.

In the app: Rekordbox → Cue Studio

Playlist Builder

Playlists built from your tags.

Pick the genres and tags you want, check the tree, then apply. DJOPS replaces only the playlists it made before and never touches yours.

  • Afro all
  • Afro tags
    • Afro latin
    • Afro tribal
  • Chill all
  • My Tags
    • psy all
    • psy tags
      • psy prog

Illustration of the tree it builds. Names stay short enough for older CDJ screens.

In the app: Rekordbox → Playlist Builder

Audio Quality

Find the weak files. Upgrade them.

It checks that each file will play on your decks and that the audio is what the file claims. Upgrade Tracks then swaps in a better download and keeps the track's rating, tags and playlists.

  • A 96 kHz master on an older club playerMay not load
  • A .wav that came out of a small MP3Fake lossless
  • A squashed, clipped masterClipping

Illustration: the kinds of finding it reports, not real results.

In the app: Rekordbox → Audio Quality, then Upgrade Tracks

Set Analyzer

A read of last night's set.

It reads the set's loudness and drops. Then it writes up the energy arc, the strongest transitions, the weak points and the crowd moments, with the AI you connect.

Illustration: energy across a set, with the drops it finds.

In the app: Analysis → Set Analyzer

Channel Insights, Doctor and Update

What worked. What to fix.

Insights answers your questions from your real numbers. Doctor answers the ones you did not ask, then ranks what to do first.

Channel Update brings what you already published up to your channel standard: titles, descriptions, tags and covers, on YouTube and SoundCloud.

Where do my viewers come from?

Illustration. Insights answers a question from your numbers. Doctor grades the rest and ranks what to do. Insights and Doctor write nothing to a channel.

In the app: Channel Studio → Channel Insights, Channel Doctor and Channel Update

Schedule and Publish

Out to every platform.

Publish Set sends the full set to YouTube and SoundCloud. Schedule Clips lines up clips for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. You can choose to review anything before it goes public.

Live with Ableton

See what your looping set is doing.

A black panel for the gig, readable from a step away: which layers are looping, what each pad and knob on your MPK does in the bank you are on, and the key, tempo and recording. It shows when Ableton Link is on, and then the CDJs set the tempo. Today it is built around one Ableton looping set and an MPK controller.

  • GroovePLAY
  • BassDUB
  • TonalREC
  • VoiceSTOP

Bank A plays the loops: REC/PLAY, STOP, CLEAR, UNDO, HALF, REVERSE, CUT, DOUBLE.

Bank B plays the effects: freeze, THIN, KILL, WASH, ROLL.

Illustration of the panel, not a real night. It opens in the app, or inside Ableton as a plugin.

In the app: Live → Ableton Link

Track Hub

From a Spotify playlist to tracks you own.

Export a Spotify playlist and DJOPS builds a Beatport playlist of the songs you do not own yet, ready to buy. For the misses it searches Bandcamp, iTunes and SoundCloud. Send any Rekordbox playlist to Spotify, the ones DJOPS builds too: test a set's flow, get a feel for it, or keep listening away from the decks. You do the buying.

SpotifyExport a playlist. Hear your Rekordbox playlists on Spotify.
BeatportA buy list of songs you do not ownYour Beatport login
The restBandcamp · iTunes · SoundCloud

Illustration of the flow. DJOPS builds the lists and never buys for you.

In the app: Track Hub → Spotify, Beatport and the other sources
