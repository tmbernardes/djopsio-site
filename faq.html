Recording and setup

What cameras work: phone, action cam, 360?

Any camera that records a standard video file works: phones (iPhone HDR included), action cams, DSLR and mirrorless. You can add several cameras to one set.

360 cameras work when the file is an exported 360 video. Export it from the camera maker's own app first (for Insta360, Insta360's app). A raw two-lens .insv file is refused unless it is tagged as 360.

Do I need the mixer recording, and how does the video sync to it?

No. A clean recording from your mixer or recorder is optional, and it gives cleaner sound. Without one, the video uses a camera's own sound.

With one, DJOPS masters it and lays it over the cameras. It matches the audio to find where each camera started, even when a camera began a couple of minutes early. If a match is off, you can line up the waves by hand, and a manual alignment is never overwritten.

How long does it take to turn a set into a video and clips?

It depends on the set's length, how many cameras you add and your Mac, and we have not published a time.

The video work is the heavy part, and 360 footage is the heaviest. A run goes step by step in a progress window, and you review the result when it is done. We would rather say that than quote minutes we have not measured on your kind of set.

Clips and posting

How many clips do I get from one set, and how are the moments picked?

You choose the number. A new setup makes 2 clips and 1 recap per set, and you can ask for more.

DJOPS finds the moments from the music: builds and drops, how hard each drop lands, and how loud the crowd is. The standard ranking weighs 70% drop strength and 30% what is on screen (faces turned to the camera) plus crowd loudness. You can tick or untick any moment before the clips are cut.

DJOPS picks moments. It cannot promise views.

Can DJOPS post without me?

Yes, if you set it to. You can choose to review anything before it goes public.

For each destination you choose how a run publishes: Wait to publish, Auto publish, or Auto publish & replace. Auto publish creates posts private or scheduled, never public on the spot.

One limit to know: Publer cannot change the time of a post it already holds. To move a scheduled Instagram or TikTok post, DJOPS has to delete it and create it again, and that is a step you confirm.

Which platforms can it post to?

YouTube (full sets and Shorts) and SoundCloud directly. Instagram Reels and TikTok through Publer.

Publer is a scheduling service you connect once. Its API access is a Business or Enterprise feature on Publer's side, so you need a Publer plan that includes it to schedule clips to Instagram and TikTok. Shorts go straight to the set's own YouTube channel; Publer takes a Short only when the channel cannot.

These are the platforms built today.

What should I post after a gig, and how often?

That is your call, and DJOPS does not promise growth or bookings.

It gives you a starting rhythm: by default one clip every 2 days, at the scheduler's publish time. You can change the spacing, the days and the time of day, and every clip post sits on one calendar you can read by week or month.

Copyright

Will my sets and clips get copyright claims on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok?

They can, and on a full DJ set a claim is likely. DJOPS does not clear music rights and cannot stop a claim.

A claim is not a strike. Usually the rights holder earns from the video or limits it, and a claim can still block a video in some countries (YouTube Help). We have not checked what every platform does with a claimed short clip, so check the platform you post to.

What DJOPS does: it does not try to hide music from matching, and it does not check whether a platform has matched your upload. Clip captions leave out track names unless you turn that on. For YouTube, Publish can add a short Content ID note to the video description; it stays off until you set one up.

General information, not legal advice.

Can I post a DJ mix on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or SoundCloud?

You can upload a mix to any of them, but whether it stays up is decided by the platform and the music's rights holders, not by DJOPS.

DJOPS does not license music and cannot prevent a takedown. YouTube often applies Content ID claims to DJ mixes, and SoundCloud can take a mix down for copyright (their help article). Many DJs put full sets on services that hold music licences for DJ mixes, such as Mixcloud. DJOPS does not publish to Mixcloud.

General information, not legal advice.

Your Rekordbox library

Will DJOPS change or mess up my Rekordbox library?

It changes your library only when you press a button that writes, and it backs up the Rekordbox database first.

The library tools (Tag Pilot, Cue Studio, Organize Library, Event Folders, Playlist Builder, Convert, Import) write to your real Rekordbox library: genre, My Tags, comments, hot cues, playlists and file paths. The video, clips and posting side never touches it.

Before each write, DJOPS saves a restore point of the Rekordbox database, and of your exported library XML when you have one. Roll back from Backups, Rekordbox Recovery. Tag Pilot and Organize Library show a preview before anything is written. Rekordbox has to be closed while DJOPS writes.

Two limits. The backup covers the Rekordbox database, not your music files. And Tag Pilot also writes tags into your audio files by default; you can switch that off, and those file edits are not backed up.

Is my library backed up?

Three things, each doing a different job.

  • Rekordbox restore points. Before each write DJOPS saves a restore point of the Rekordbox database, and of your exported library XML when you have one. Roll the library and its XML back together from Backups, Rekordbox Recovery. They cover the database, not your music files.
  • Google Drive. It uploads the folders you choose, and only what is new or changed. DJOPS can also keep a Drive copy of each set's final mix.
  • An external drive. The backup moves your recordings off the Mac, and deletes each Mac copy only once the drive's copy matches in size and checksum. A recording that does not match stays on the Mac. Your music, Rekordbox library and media kit are copied, never deleted.

Clip files for posting can live on your Google Drive or on Cloudflare R2. That is where posting reads them from, not a backup. Your data folder (sets, settings and logs) is not part of the drive backup.

What do the Rekordbox tools do to my tags, cues and files?

Each tool shows what it will change and writes after a backup.

Tag Pilot writes genre, comments and My Tags to Rekordbox. Anything you wrote in a comment stays: DJOPS replaces only its own part, so a re-tag never stacks a second note. By default it also writes the tags into the audio files: full tags in MP3, AIFF, FLAC and M4A, and a comment only in WAV. Switch that off and only Rekordbox changes.

Cue Studio suggests hot cues placed on bar starts. Tracks that already have hot cues are flagged and skipped by default.

Convert Library Files makes new FLAC, WAV, AIFF or MP3 files and points Rekordbox at them. The old files stay until you press Delete old files, and Undo last conversion points Rekordbox back.

Does it work with Serato, Traktor or Engine, or only Rekordbox?

The library tools work with Rekordbox only. Serato, Traktor and Engine libraries are not supported today.

The video, clips and posting side needs no DJ software. It works from your camera files and audio recordings, whichever software you play on.

Privacy and AI

Does my footage leave my Mac?

Your recordings, library and set files stay on your Mac. They leave only when you publish or connect a service, and only to that service.

  • Uploads and posts you start: YouTube, SoundCloud, and clips with their captions to Publer.
  • Google Drive, only if you turn on backups or the Drive copy of the final mix.
  • A cloud AI, only if you connect one. The text it needs (a tracklist, a draft description) goes straight from your Mac to that provider, and hooks and captions can also send a few pictures from the clip.
  • Sign-ins, shop lookups (Beatport, Spotify), and a one-time download of the small face and crowd models.

The app does not collect analytics today. If usage or crash reporting is ever added it will be opt-in (Privacy Policy).

Does it work offline?

Mostly, yes. Your sets, clips and Rekordbox library live on your Mac, so most of the app runs with no internet. Only a step that talks to another service needs a connection, and it says so in plain words when it cannot reach that service.

Works with no internet
  • Adding and preparing a set from files on your Mac, the Video Mixer, clips and the recap clip, and covers made from your own footage.
  • The library tools: organizing, converting, checking audio quality, importing and cue editing.
Waits for the internet
  • Sign-ins, which open the provider's own page.
  • Uploads and posting: YouTube, SoundCloud and the scheduler that posts your clips.
  • Google Drive: backups, the Drive copy of the final mix and files you pick from Drive.
  • Shop and playlist lookups in Track Hub and Beatport.
  • Channel Studio, which reads and edits your YouTube channel.
  • The cloud AIs: Claude, OpenAI and Gemini.

When a run was made without the internet or without AI, the job window shows a gray note, so a gap never passes quietly. A one-time download of the small face and crowd models is the one thing a fresh install fetches; until then covers judge frames by light alone.

Do I need AI?

No. Every step works without AI. Cutting the video and clips, covers, the Video Mixer, cue suggestions and the library tools run on your Mac with no AI.

Where a step is sharper with an AI, it says so. With none connected, hooks and captions are plain ones written from the set's details, Plan Next Set is built from your dropdowns, and the set analysis shows its tables without the written notes. The job window shows a gray note when a run was made without AI.

Four are supported: Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI and Gemini with your own key, and LM Studio, which runs a model on your Mac with no key and no internet. Gemini has a free tier. Titles, descriptions, tracklist photos, reports, channel questions and Tag Pilot need one of them: with none, they say why and write nothing.

What does the AI do, and is my content used to train it?

AI writes titles, descriptions, hooks and captions, and helps tag tracks. You connect your own AI key (Gemini has a free tier; OpenAI and Anthropic charge per use), or run a model on your Mac with LM Studio.

DJOPS does not use your data to train AI models. A provider's own training terms are theirs, and we cannot promise them, so read the terms of the provider you pick.

AI is optional for hooks and captions: with no AI connected, DJOPS writes plain ones from the set's details and says so. Titles, descriptions, tracklist photos and channel reports need an AI.

Beta and price

Is it free? What will it cost after the beta?

It is free during the beta. Nothing in the beta app charges you.

After launch, DJOPS stays free to use. You see every result first, and you pay only when you export or publish. We have not set prices, so we do not quote any.

Other companies' costs are separate: AI providers (Gemini has a free tier), and a Publer plan with API access if you post to Instagram and TikTok.

How does the beta work: who gets in, and when?

You request beta access with a short form, and we let DJs in a few at a time. We choose, so a request is not a guaranteed place.

We do not publish a headcount or a date. The form asks what you play, and we use your answers only to contact you about the beta (Privacy Policy). It is a beta: expect rough edges, and tell us what you hit.

What happens to my videos if the beta ends or I stop using it?

Your videos and clips are normal files in your set folders on your Mac, and posts you already published stay on their platforms.

We have not published what happens to beta access at launch beyond the pricing above.

Requirements

What Mac do I need?

A Mac running macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Apple Silicon is recommended.

Only the library and plan tools need Rekordbox; video and clips work from your camera and audio files. By default DJOPS links to your recordings instead of copying them, so adding a file takes no extra disk, but masters, renders and clips do. We have not published a disk figure per set.

Is it Mac only?

Yes. DJOPS is a Mac app, and there is no Windows version. We have not set a date for one.
